Dangerous products or services

Google Ads restricts the promotion of products or services that can cause damage, harm, or injury. Use this guide to find the relevant category, understand what is allowed or not allowed under the stated rule, and open the detailed policy when you need more information.

At a glance
  1. Select a category from the contents list.
  2. Look for the status beside each rule.
  3. Always review the linked policy for the full requirements.

Guns, gun parts, and related products

4 rules3 not allowed1 allowed
  • ✓
    AllowedAds for gun parts and associated items that increase the safety of a gun.
  • ✕
    Not allowedAds for functional devices that appear to discharge a projectile at high velocity, whether for sport, self-defense, or combat.
  • ✕

Recreational drugs

5 rules4 not allowed1 conditional
  • !
    Allowed with conditionsCertain topical, hemp-derived cannabidiol (CBD) products with THC content of 0.3% or less may be advertised only if the advertiser applies for the required authorization and targets California, Colorado, and Puerto Rico. Some ad formats may have additional restrictions.
  • ✕
    Not allowedAds for substances that alter mental state for recreational purposes or induce highs are not allowed.
  • ✕
    Not allowedAds for products or services marketed as facilitating recreational drug use.
  • ✕
    Not allowedAds for instructional content about producing, purchasing, or using recreational drugs.
  • ✕
    Not allowedAds for pill presses, encapsulating machines, and related components used to compact or fill powders, granules, or other materials into tablets or capsules.
    Examples of restricted equipment: Pill presses, tableting machines, dies, molds, stamps, and punches used to create or imprint pills and tablets.
